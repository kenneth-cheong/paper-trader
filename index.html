Auto-trading rules

Rules buy and sell for you when their condition is met. They run while this page is open, and when you come back they replay the last 5 trading days of 15-minute prices, so a rule still fires at the time and price it would have. Rules only manage long holdings; they never open shorts.

AI strategist

Claude reads the last year of prices, today's news and your own trades, then proposes strategies as auto-trading rules. Each one is backtested on the past year before you decide to use it. Runs with your Anthropic API key.

Trade history

Virtual money only; nothing here is financial advice. Prices come from Yahoo Finance via a scheduled job and may be delayed. Your portfolio and rules are saved in this browser; use Settings to export them or move them to another device.
